# Adapt guard and report

## What you may take from a winner

- The **structure**: how it opens (question, bold claim, small scene, number first), how many beats, how long it is, how it ends (open question, plain takeaway, nothing), whether it used a list.
- The **format**: one image, a carousel, a text-only post, a short thread.
- The **timing** of the topic: that people care about it now.

## What you may not take

- Wording. No run of five or more words in a row from the source. Do not paraphrase sentence by sentence either: if you could lay the two posts side by side and map them line to line, the rewrite is too close.
- Their numbers, names, customers, quotes, screenshots or story.
- Their personal experience. "When I was building X, I..." is the author's, not ours.
- Their conclusion, if it is a claim about the world that we cannot source.

## The invented-claims guard

Every sentence of the adapted post must pass one of three tests:

1. **Fact:** it is on the user's facts file. Cite the line.
2. **Opinion:** it is clearly the author's view and the author confirmed they hold it. Mark it.
3. **Structure:** it carries no claim at all (a transition, a question, a greeting).

A sentence that is none of these is deleted or sent back as a question to the author. Show the table:

| Sentence in the post | Test | Fact line / note |
|---|---|---|
| "Saved carts are live." | Fact | F3: shipped 3 March, changelog |
| "Come back tomorrow, the cart is waiting." | Structure | no claim |
| "Shoppers kept asking for this." | none | not in facts: removed |

Specific traps:

- The winner opens with a story. We have no such story. Pick a structure that does not need one.
- The winner shows a result with a number. We have no such number. Write the post without a number, or do not use this winner.
- The winner is a hot take about a competitor. Use it only if the claim is confirmed by an official source, and then only as one of the 1-in-4-or-5 trend posts.
- The winner says "everyone is talking about X". That is a claim about the world. Cite the evidence from this research, with the sample size, or leave it out.

If a winner cannot be adapted without breaking the guard, say so and move on to the next one.

## Report

```
Niche trends: <niche>
Window: <start> to <end> (today <date>)

Coverage
- X: <n kept> of <n collected>   confidence: <normal | low>
- Bluesky: ...
- Reddit: ...
- Hacker News: ...
- Instagram: ...
Dropped: off-niche <n>, rumour <n>, internals <n>, drama <n>, bait <n>, ad <n>, duplicate <n>, format <n>

Winners (score, network, link)
1. <score> <network> <link>
   Numbers: <raw engagement>, sample <n>
   Topic: <one line>
   Structure: <opening / beats / length / ending / image>
   Why it probably worked: <one honest line, marked as a guess>

Patterns (need 3+ items from 2+ authors)
- <pattern>: <n items>, <links>

Not enough evidence
- <what could not be concluded and why>

Angles from our facts (only from the facts file)
- <angle> uses F2, F5

Adapted drafts (only if asked)
- <draft>, with the sentence-to-fact table
```

An empty month looks like this: "Nothing cleared the bar on any network. Coverage was thin: X 14 items, Bluesky 6. Not enough to name a pattern."
